You’re invited to the Modwerk beta

Hey guys,

the past week I’ve been working on a big Octatrack update, and I’d love for you to try it before it’s out for everyone.

- Flash modules over USB
  Install the Modwerk base once from the card, then connect directly via USB – no card, no reboot.
- All modules work together
  All 19 can be on your Octatrack at the same time, including the beta-only POLY8 and Air Chorus.
- Smarter DSP use
  FX get loaded onto the DSP chip only when a track uses them, and tracks on the same core share one copy of the code.
- A status page for your Octatrack
  What’s on it, its CPU and DSP load, and the last self-test.

You’ll need an Octatrack MKII, your OS 1.40C file, a USB cable and Chrome or Edge on a computer. The beta is limited to 50 people.

Join the beta (https://modwerk.app/#beta/join/00000000000000000000000000000000)

It’s still a beta and not safe to use in a live setting (yet) – you’ll find the details and risks on the site. Let me know what you think in the development Discord!

Thank you,
Jannik
